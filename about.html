About Me

I’m a cloud engineering leader and infrastructure specialist with 25+ years in IT and over a decade focused on building and scaling cloud platforms in AWS and GCP. My background spans everything from traditional data center environments to modern, automated, multi-cloud architectures—giving me a practical, end-to-end perspective on how to evolve technology organizations. In my current role at Telus Health, I lead a team of cloud engineers and administrators responsible for designing, building, and operating enterprise-scale cloud platforms. I work closely with product, security, and operations teams to enable secure, scalable, and cost-efficient solutions, while helping drive cloud adoption across the organization. A big part of my focus is creating well-architected landing zones, standardizing infrastructure through IaC, and enabling teams to move faster through automation and DevOps practices. I’ve led large-scale cloud migrations, built AWS Organizations and Control Tower environments from the ground up, and implemented CI/CD pipelines and platform services that support multiple product teams. My approach blends hands-on technical depth with leadership—mentoring engineers, setting standards, and ensuring long-term sustainability of the platform. Across my career, I’ve consistently worked at the intersection of infrastructure, networking, and systems engineering, with deep expertise in Linux, Windows, identity, networking, and high-availability environments. That foundation allows me to design cloud solutions that are not just modern, but reliable, secure, and aligned to real business needs. I’m particularly interested in roles and initiatives focused on platform engineering, cloud governance, and building scalable foundations that empower development teams.
